Creta vs XUV400.

A petrol-and-diesel SUV against a 4.2 m electric one. The tables make the price gap obvious; they also show where both manufacturers leave blanks.

Petrol / diesel vs electric₹10.91–20.46 lakh vs ₹17.49 lakhNovember 2026

The case for each vehicle

Case for the Creta

  • Three engines and four gearbox choices, up to 160 PS and 253 Nm.
  • Six airbags and a full stability suite standard; Level 2 ADAS on higher trims.
  • No current Bharat NCAP rating and no official efficiency, clearance, kerb or boot figures.

Case for the XUV400

  • Five-star Bharat NCAP adult and child ratings, tested in 2024.
  • 456 km MIDC from 39.4 kWh, with 110 kW and 310 Nm.
  • One variant only, and 50 kW DC charging is modest for the price.

Price context: the Creta spans a much wider band because it has three engines and eight trim levels. The XUV400's ₹17.49 lakh is a single-variant figure, and Mahindra's configurator does not name the city.

Safety context: the Creta's Global NCAP result is for a previous two-airbag car. The XUV400's five-star result is current Bharat NCAP testing, but curtain airbags were optional on the tested family.

Data gaps: both manufacturers leave ground clearance and kerb weight unpublished. Hyundai also does not publish ARAI efficiency, boot volume or a current NCAP rating. No secondary figures are substituted.
