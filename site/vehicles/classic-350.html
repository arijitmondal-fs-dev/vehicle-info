The brief

The Classic 350 is Royal Enfield's retro-standard, powered by a 349 cc air-oil-cooled single producing 20.2 bhp and 27 Nm through a five-speed gearbox with an assist-and-slipper clutch. It pairs a twin-downtube spine frame with 41 mm forks and twin emulsion shocks.

Most colours get dual-channel ABS; the Redditch Red and Halcyon Black use single-channel ABS. For 2026 the bike carries LED lighting, Tripper turn-by-turn navigation, USB-C charging and adjustable levers as standard. At 195 kg, it is the heaviest machine in the motorcycle pair.

What the numbers say

These are observations from the published figures, not road-test conclusions.

Strengths

  • 27 Nm at 4,000 rpm gives strong low-end pull for a 349 cc single.
  • An 805 mm seat and 170 mm clearance make it easier to live with than its weight suggests.
  • LED lighting, Tripper navigation, USB-C and adjustable levers are standard.

Trade-offs

  • At 195 kg with a 1,390 mm wheelbase, it is cumbersome in dense traffic.
  • It costs roughly ₹1.09 lakh more than the Splendor Plus at entry.
  • No official fuel-efficiency figure, so running costs cannot be quoted from a manufacturer source.
