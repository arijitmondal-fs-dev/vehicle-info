The brief

The India-market 390 Duke uses a 349.32 cc liquid-cooled single, not the 398.7 cc engine sold in some international markets. It makes 41.5 PS at 8,600 rpm and 33.5 Nm at 7,000 rpm, with a 168.3 kg kerb weight and a 15 L tank.

The standard electronics package is unusually complete for the price: cornering ABS, SuperMoto ABS, traction control, launch control, a bi-directional quickshifter, cruise control and a 5-inch Bluetooth TFT. KTM India does not publish length, width, height or wheelbase for this model.

What the numbers say

These are observations from the published figures, not road-test conclusions.

Strengths

  • 41.5 PS from 349.32 cc with a 168.3 kg kerb weight.
  • Cornering ABS, SuperMoto ABS, traction control, launch control, quickshifter and cruise control are standard.
  • Adjustable suspension at both ends and a 800/820 mm seat height.

Trade-offs

  • Less torque than the Himalayan 450: 33.5 Nm at 7,000 rpm against 40 Nm at 5,500 rpm.
  • Road-biased 17-inch wheels and 183 mm clearance limit off-road use.
  • No official ARAI figure and no published length, width, height or wheelbase.
