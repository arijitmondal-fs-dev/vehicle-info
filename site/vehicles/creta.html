The brief

The 2026 Creta is a five-seat SUV offered with three engines: a 1.5 L petrol, a 1.5 L diesel and a 1.5 L turbo-petrol. The petrol and diesel come with manual or automatic gearboxes; the turbo-petrol is a 7DCT only.

Hyundai publishes engine outputs and dimensions but not ARAI efficiency, ground clearance, kerb weight or boot volume for the current car. Six airbags, ESC, hill-start assist and ISOFIX are standard, with Level 2 ADAS on higher trims. The only NCAP result is a 2022 Global NCAP 3-star rating for the previous two-airbag version.

Data cut-off: 3 November 2026. Prices are ex-showroom Delhi and exclude insurance, registration and offers.

What the numbers say

These are observations from the published figures, not road-test conclusions.

Strengths

  • Three engines and four gearbox choices, from 115 PS petrol to 160 PS turbo-petrol.
  • Six airbags and a full stability and hill-assist suite are standard across the range.
  • Top trims carry Level 2 ADAS, panoramic sunroof, ventilated seats, Bose audio and surround-view cameras.

Trade-offs

  • No Bharat NCAP rating; the only official result is a 3-star rating for the old two-airbag car.
  • The top diesel reaches ₹20.46 lakh ex-showroom.
  • Hyundai does not publish ARAI efficiency, ground clearance, kerb weight or boot volume.
