Space, polish and a hybrid option.

The brief

The fifth-generation City is the largest car in this set: 4,594 mm long, with a 2,600 mm wheelbase and a 506 L boot. Honda offers a 1.5 L petrol engine with a six-speed manual or CVT, and a strong-hybrid e:HEV with an e-CVT.

The petrol makes 89 kW and 145 Nm. The hybrid combines a 74 kW Atkinson-cycle engine with an 80 kW traction motor for a 93 kW system output and 253 Nm from the motor. Honda SENSING ADAS is available from the V grade; the hybrid is sold only as the top ZX+.

Data cut-off: 3 November 2026. Prices are ex-showroom Delhi and exclude insurance, registration and offers.

What the numbers say

These are observations from the published figures, not road-test conclusions.

Strengths

  • The 2,600 mm wheelbase and 506 L boot give it far more space than the Swift.
  • The e:HEV combines 253 Nm of motor torque with a 27.26 km/l ARAI figure.
  • Honda SENSING ADAS is available from the V grade, not just the top trim.

Trade-offs

  • The starting price is roughly double the Swift's, and the hybrid is ZX+ only at ₹21.36 lakh.
  • Honda does not publish ground clearance for the India car.
  • No current Bharat NCAP rating; the 4-star result is for the previous generation.
