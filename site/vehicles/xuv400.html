The brief

The XUV400 is Mahindra's 4.2 m electric SUV. The current configurator lists a single variant, the EL Pro ST with a 39.4 kWh battery and 7.2 kW AC charger, at ₹17.49 lakh ex-showroom.

A 110 kW motor drives the front wheels through a single-speed automatic, with 310 Nm and a claimed 0–100 km/h time of 8.3 seconds. The 39.4 kWh version has a 456 km MIDC range. Bharat NCAP awarded five stars for adult and child protection in 2024; Mahindra does not publish ground clearance or kerb weight.

Data cut-off: 3 November 2026. Prices are ex-showroom Delhi and exclude insurance, registration and offers.

What the numbers say

These are observations from the published figures, not road-test conclusions.

Strengths

  • Five-star Bharat NCAP adult and child ratings, tested in 2024.
  • 456 km MIDC range from the 39.4 kWh battery, with 110 kW and 310 Nm.
  • 7.2 kW AC charging in 6.5 hours and 50 kW DC to 80% in 50 minutes.

Trade-offs

  • Only one variant is currently orderable, at ₹17.49 lakh ex-showroom.
  • Mahindra does not publish ground clearance or kerb weight.
  • 50 kW DC charging is modest against newer electric SUVs.
