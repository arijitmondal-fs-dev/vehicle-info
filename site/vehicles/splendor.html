The brief

The Splendor Plus is Hero's volume commuter: a 97.2 cc air-cooled single with fuel injection, a four-speed gearbox and a 112 kg kerb weight. Hero quotes 5.9 kW at 8,000 rpm and 8.05 Nm at 6,000 rpm across the range.

The mechanical package is deliberately simple. The XTEC and XTEC 2.0 trims add digital instrumentation, Bluetooth alerts, LED lighting and a charging port, but the engine, frame and brakes are shared. It is the cheapest way into this set, and the easiest to handle in city traffic.

What the numbers say

These are observations from the published figures, not road-test conclusions.

Strengths

  • Lowest price and lowest running-cost claim in this set, at ₹78,912 and 70 km/l.
  • A 112 kg kerb weight and 785 mm seat height make it very manageable in traffic.
  • Five-year / 70,000 km warranty is the longest cover in the motorcycle pair.

Trade-offs

  • 97.2 cc, about 7.9 bhp and four gears limit highway and two-up cruising.
  • No ABS anywhere in the range; braking assistance is IBS only.
  • The 9.8 L tank and compact dimensions restrict long-distance touring.
