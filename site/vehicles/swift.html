The brief

The Epic New Swift is Maruti Suzuki's compact hatchback, built around a 1,197 cc three-cylinder petrol engine and a light 924 kg kerb weight. It is the cheapest car in this set and the shortest, at 3,860 mm.

The range runs from the LXi to the ZXi+ dual-tone, with five-speed manual and AMT gearboxes. Six airbags, electronic stability program and hill-hold assist are standard across the range. The top trim adds a 22.86 cm touchscreen, cruise control and a reverse camera.

Data cut-off: 3 November 2026. Prices are ex-showroom Delhi and exclude insurance, registration and offers.

What the numbers say

These are observations from the published figures, not road-test conclusions.

Strengths

  • Lowest entry price in the set, at ₹5,83,900 ex-showroom Delhi.
  • Light 924 kg kerb weight and a 3,860 mm length make it easy in city traffic.
  • Six airbags, ESP and hill-hold assist are standard even on the base LXi.

Trade-offs

  • The 1,197 cc three-cylinder engine makes 60–61 kW, well below the City's 89 kW.
  • The 265 L boot and 2,450 mm wheelbase limit rear-seat and luggage space.
  • No current Bharat NCAP rating; the last tested two-airbag Swift scored one star.
