02 / Dimension study

Numbers you can stand next to.

Wheelbase, seat height and ground clearance are easier to judge as a drawing than as a table row. Each vehicle file includes a side-profile schematic with only the dimensions the manufacturer publishes.

Where a figure is missing, the drawing says so. It is not filled in from a dealer brochure or an aggregator.

03 / Method

How these files are built.

Every file starts with the manufacturer's own page, brochure or specification sheet. Nothing is estimated to fill a gap.

  1. Collect the primary sourceOfficial India pages, price lists, brochures and owner's manuals. Global NCAP and Bharat NCAP are used only for safety ratings.
  2. Record conflicts, not averagesIf a manufacturer publishes two figures, both appear in the file. The page says which one is used for the headline.
  3. Leave gaps visibleUnpublished dimensions and efficiency figures are marked “not published”. They are never replaced with a plausible number.
  4. Date the dataPrices and specifications are a November 2026 snapshot. Every source is linked at the bottom of its file.
Ex-showroom
The price before insurance, registration and road tax. It is not the on-road price.
Kerb weight
The vehicle with fluids and a defined fuel load, but without a rider or luggage.
Wheelbase
The distance between front and rear axle centres. It shapes cabin space and straight-line stability.
Claimed efficiency
A manufacturer or ARAI test figure. Real-world consumption depends on speed, load and conditions.
